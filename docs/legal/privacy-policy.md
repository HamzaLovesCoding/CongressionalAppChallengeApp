## The short version

- Lane has no accounts right now. Your lessons, XP, streak, daily goal, likes, saves and Garage cars are saved **only on your device**.
- We can't see that information. We don't sell it, share it or use it for ads.
- Lane has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. It never asks for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
- You can erase everything at any time by clearing Lane's site data or removing the app from your home screen.
- Sign-in and online leaderboards are planned. Before they launch, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app.

## 2. What Lane saves on your device

Lane uses your browser's storage to remember your progress between visits. This information stays on your phone, tablet or computer. It is never sent to us.

| What | Why Lane keeps it |
| --- | --- |
| Which lessons you finished, how many tries each took, and whether your first answer was right | To show your progress, the next lesson, milestones and first-try accuracy |
| XP earned on each day | For your total XP, streak, weekly bars, daily goal and the weekly leaderboard |
| Your daily goal (10, 30 or 50 XP) | To show your goal ring |
| Lessons you liked or saved | To show them on your Profile |
| Garage cars you own and the one you picked, and the XP you spent on them | For the Garage and the car on your Progress road. Avatar picks from earlier versions of Lane are kept too, so nothing you earned is lost |
| Whether sound is on or off in the driving simulator | To remember your choice |
| Lane's own files and lesson videos you've watched | So Lane opens fast and works offline |

**Practice test answers are not saved.** They're cleared as soon as you leave the test.

Anyone who uses the same device and browser can see this progress. If you share a device, keep that in mind.

## 5. Sharing a lesson

The share button opens your device's share menu, or copies a link if no menu is available. The link only names the lesson, for example `#/feed/high-beams`. It contains nothing about you or your progress, and we can't see who you share it with.

## 6. Links to other sites

Each video lesson's **About this lesson** sheet links to the original video on YouTube. If you open that link, you leave Lane, and YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

## 7. Performance test mode

Adding `?perf=1` to Lane's address turns on a testing overlay for the driving simulator. It shows device measurements (browser, screen size and frame rate) on your own screen. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you copy it yourself.

## 8. How to delete your data

Because everything is stored on your device, you control it:

- **iPhone or iPad (Safari):** open Settings → Safari (on newer versions, Settings → Apps → Safari) → Advanced → Website Data, find Lane's site and delete it. If you added Lane to your Home Screen, it keeps its own copy of your progress; deleting the Home Screen icon removes it.
- **Android (Chrome):** Chrome → Settings → Site settings → All sites, find Lane's site and tap **Delete data**.
- **Computer:** clear site data for Lane in your browser's settings.

This permanently erases your lessons, XP, streak and Garage. We can't recover them, because we never had a copy.
