## 2. What Lane is, and what it isn't

Lane is a free learning app with short driving lessons, quick questions, a practice test and an optional driving simulator.

Lane is **not**:

- a driver's education or driver's training course
- the California Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV), or connected with it
- an official DMV knowledge test. Lane's practice test is practice only
- a way to earn a permit, a license or course credit

Doing well in Lane doesn't mean you'll pass the permit test, and it doesn't mean you're ready to drive. Only real, supervised practice and the DMV can decide that.

## 3. Never use Lane while driving

Don't watch lessons, answer questions or use the simulator while you're driving or while a vehicle you're operating is moving. This includes when you're practicing with a learner's permit. Pay full attention to the road. Pull over safely first, or let a passenger use Lane.

## 4. Driving information in Lane

- **The handbook is the source of truth.** Lane's video lessons cite the *California Quick Reference Driver's Handbook (DL 600X, 2025)* by section and page. The DMV's official handbook and the California Vehicle Code control, not Lane.
- **Some questions are still being reviewed.** Lane's lesson questions are checked against the handbook by a person before they're final. Some are still being checked, and mistakes are possible.
- **Laws change.** Rules can change after a lesson was written. Lane's information may be out of date.
- **Other places have other rules.** Lane teaches California rules. Other states and countries may differ.
- **Report mistakes.** If you think a lesson or answer is wrong, tell us at hamza.w.3790@gmail.com. A wrong answer is the most serious problem Lane can have, and we'll fix it.

Lane gives general learning information. It isn't legal advice.

## 5. Lesson videos

Lesson videos are made by independent creators and are used in Lane with permission or under their licenses. Each video lesson credits and links to its original video under **About this lesson**.

Some videos were filmed outside California and show left-hand traffic or a right-hand-drive car. Those lessons say so in the California rule dropdown on the video. Always follow the California rule shown.

You may watch the videos in Lane. Don't download, copy or re-upload them. They belong to their creators.

## 6. Local safety figures

Some lessons mention crash figures for Novato, California, from the California Office of Traffic Safety (2023). They describe fatal-and-injury crashes only, not every crash, and they don't say who was at fault. They're there to show why the rules matter.

## 7. The driving simulator

The simulator is optional practice. It's a simplified 3D scene, not a real car or real driving conditions. How you do in it doesn't measure your driving skill and doesn't count toward finishing a lesson.

## 8. XP, streaks, milestones and Garage cars

- They're virtual rewards for learning. They have **no money value**.
- They can't be bought, sold, traded or exchanged for anything outside Lane. Lane has no real-money purchases.
- Spending XP on a Garage car doesn't lower your total XP or your streak.
- Today they're saved only on your device. Clearing your browser data, or switching devices, erases them, and we can't restore them.
- We may change how rewards work, for example when new lessons are added. We won't take away rewards you've already earned, except XP earned by breaking these terms.
